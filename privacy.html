1. Who we are

My Nudge is developed and published by Ranjith Arimbra, an independent developer based in India (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains what information the My Nudge app (“the app”) handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

2. Information stored on your device

When you use the app, it saves the following information in the app’s private storage on your device:

  • Drink records — the amount and time of each drink you log.
  • Daily goal — the intake target you set.
  • Reminder settings — when and how often you want to be reminded.
  • App preferences — settings such as units and display options.

This information never leaves your device through the app. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to see it.

3. Information we do not collect

The app does not collect, receive or store on any server:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details (the app has no accounts);
  • your location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone;
  • your advertising ID or any other device identifier;
  • usage statistics, analytics, crash reports or diagnostics.

The app contains no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics code.

4. How the app uses your information

The information described in section 2 is used only on your device, and only to:

  • show your progress towards your daily goal and your recent history;
  • schedule and show the reminders you have set;
  • remember your settings between sessions.

We do not use your information for profiling, automated decision-making, advertising or any other purpose.

5. Information handled outside the app

A few things happen outside the app. In each case, the provider handles information under its own privacy policy, not this one.

If you email us

If you contact us at mynudgesupport@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and resolve your request. We keep support emails for up to 12 months after your request is resolved, unless we need to keep them longer to comply with the law, and then delete them. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail).

Google Play

When you download or update the app, Google processes information under the Google Privacy Policy. We may see aggregated, non-identifying statistics that Google Play provides to all developers, such as install counts and ratings.

This website

This policy and our Terms are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open these pages, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not use cookies or analytics on these pages.

6. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information with anyone, and the app has no feature for uploading or sharing your records. The only information we ever hold is support email correspondence. We will disclose that only if required by law, for example in response to a valid order of a court or government authority.

7. Device permissions

The app requests only the permissions it needs to deliver reminders. It does not request internet access.

PermissionWhy it is needed
NotificationsTo show your hydration reminders. You can turn this off at any time in Android settings.
Run at startupTo restore your scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.
Alarms & remindersTo deliver reminders at the times you choose.

8. Reminders and your lock screen

Reminders are scheduled by Android on your device. Reminder text is generic and does not show your daily totals. Depending on your lock-screen settings, anyone with physical access to your phone may be able to see a reminder or tap its actions (such as Add or Snooze). You can control this in Android’s notification and lock-screen settings.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • delete individual drinks from the Today screen;
  • erase all app data using Delete all data in Settings;
  • uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, which permanently removes all of its data.

Because we never receive your app data, we cannot view, recover or delete it for you, and deleted data cannot be restored.

10. Backups and changing phones

The app does not back up your data to any server, and it does not offer export, import or cloud restore. If you change or reset your phone, your records will not be carried over.

11. Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot normally access. The app does not add its own encryption; it relies on Android’s built-in protections. To keep your data safe, use a screen lock and keep your device updated. No method of storage is completely secure, but because the app sends nothing over the internet, your records are not exposed to network-based risks from the app.

12. Children

My Nudge is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Users under 18 should use the app with the involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Your rights

Since your app data stays on your device, you already have full control over it: you can view, edit and delete it at any time, as described in section 9.

Depending on where you live — for example under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation, or US state privacy laws — you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information we hold about you, to withdraw consent, to nominate another person to exercise your rights, and to complain to a data protection authority. In practice, the only personal information we may hold is support email correspondence. To make a request, email mynudgesupport@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

14. Health information

Your drink records are kept only on your device and are never received by us. My Nudge is a general wellness tool, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice. See the health section of our Terms of Use.

15. Changes to this policy

We will review this policy before releasing any change to how the app handles information — for example, adding internet access, accounts or third-party services. When we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, mention it in the app’s update notes on Google Play. Please check this page from time to time.
