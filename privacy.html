1. Who we are

My Nudge is provided by My Nudge Admin, based in India

For privacy questions and app support, contact ranjimist@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • My Nudge is designed to log water intake and give optional reminders on your phone, without an account. The app does not request internet access and does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • Your drink records, goals and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The app has no feature that uploads them to us. Reminders and notification actions are handled by Android on your device.
  • Opening this policy in a browser, emailing us, and using Google Play involve separate services. They may process some information under their own policies, as explained in section 5.

3. Information the app stores on your device

The app stores:

  • Drink records: the amount in millilitres, the date and time, the time zone at the time you logged it, and an internal record number. Every record is saved as water; there is no drink-type choice.
  • Daily goals: each goal amount, the date it started, and an internal record number.
  • Preferences: your volume unit (ml or fl oz), whether setup is complete, whether reminders are on, the reminder interval, active hours, quiet hours and whether they are on, whether to skip reminders once your goal is met, snooze length, and the app’s built-in cup sizes. This version does not let you edit cup sizes.
  • Reminder timing: when the app last processed a reminder (whether or not it was shown) and, if you snoozed one, when it is due.

The app uses your device language and region settings to suggest a starting unit, and your device time zone to work out dates. This is not location tracking. Daily totals, progress, streaks and recent history are calculated on your device and are not stored as a separate profile.

Android also keeps notification and alarm information and short-lived screen state for the app. This is not an account with us.

Your water-intake records can reveal health-related habits. App-private storage stops other apps from reading them in normal use, but anyone using your unlocked phone may see them, and reminders may be visible on your lock screen or in screenshots, depending on your settings.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or advertising ID.

4. How the app uses this information

The app uses your records and settings on your device to save and manage your entries, goals and settings, calculate your progress and history, and schedule reminders. It does not use your records for advertising, and it does not send them to us for analysis or sale.

7. Permissions

  • Notifications: used only for optional hydration reminders. On Android 13 and later you are asked whether to allow them, and you can say no. You can turn reminders off in the app or block notifications in Android settings. Logging drinks works without notifications.
  • Restart and time events: the app listens for your phone restarting, the app being updated, and the clock or time zone changing, so that it can rebuild your reminder schedule from the settings it already stores. This does not give the app internet access or access to your contacts, location or other apps’ data.

8. Reminders and your lock screen

Reminders are scheduled on your phone through Android, not sent from a server. Their title and text are generic (“Time for a drink”) and do not show your daily totals. The buttons show a cup amount (for example “Add 250 ml”) and a snooze length.

Depending on your Android version, device and notification settings, someone with access to your lock screen may be able to see a reminder or tap Add or Snooze without opening My Nudge. Add records a drink; Snooze changes when the next reminder is due. If you don’t want this, change your lock-screen notification settings for My Nudge in Android.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

  • The app does not delete drink or goal history automatically. Records normally stay until you remove them or the app’s storage is cleared. A device fault, software error or damaged settings file can also cause information to be lost or reset.
  • You can edit the amount of, or delete, drinks shown on the Today screen. Deleting a drink briefly offers Undo. Older days can’t be edited one by one.
  • Settings › Delete all data asks you to confirm. It then cancels scheduled reminders and any current reminder, clears your drink records, goals and settings, and returns the app to setup.
  • Android normally removes the app’s data when you clear its storage or uninstall it. None of these steps guarantees secure physical erasure, and none removes copies outside the app, such as screenshots or emails you sent to support.
  • This version has no export, import or cloud restore feature. We cannot see, retrieve, restore, edit or delete your app records remotely. Deleting app data does not delete support emails or information held by the website host; see section 5.

10. Backups and changing phones

My Nudge is set up so that Android does not include its data in cloud backups or device-to-device transfers. The app has no backup, export or transfer feature of its own. Do not expect your history to move to a new phone or be recoverable after uninstalling, clearing storage, or losing, resetting or replacing your phone. Actual behaviour can vary by Android version and manufacturer tools. If you used an earlier test version with a different package name, its history does not carry over.

11. Security

Your records are kept in the app’s private storage on your phone. The app does not add its own encryption layer. Protection relies on Android’s access controls and your device’s security, such as your screen lock and device encryption where available. This cannot prevent all access, copying or changes, especially on an unlocked, rooted or compromised device. Keep your phone locked and up to date, and consider what reminders and screenshots may show. No security measure can guarantee information is completely secure.
