Privacy Policy – AquaLog

Effective date: 2 November 2026

AquaLog (“the app”) is a privacy-first water intake tracker. We designed it so that no personal or analytical data leaves your device.

What data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data.

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs
  • No internet permission — the app works fully offline
  • No cloud backup of your data

Data stored on your device

The following information is stored only in the app’s private storage on your phone:

  • Water intake entries (amount, time, optional drink type)
  • Daily goal and goal history
  • Cup presets and reminder preferences
  • Display settings (units, theme preference if set)

This data never leaves your device. Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer of app data are disabled.

Optional goal calculator

If you use the optional “Suggest a goal” calculator, any inputs (such as body weight or activity level) are used only in memory to calculate a suggestion. These values are never saved to disk, logs, or backups.

Reminders

Hydration reminders are generated locally on your device using Android’s notification system. We do not send push notifications from any server.

Your control

  • You can delete all data at any time from Settings → Delete all data.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently deletes all data with no recovery.
  • You can turn reminders on or off at any time.

Health disclaimer

AquaLog is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy
