5. Health and wellness

My Nudge is designed for general wellness tracking. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition, and it is not designed to manage a prescribed fluid plan. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

The default goal of 2,000 ml and the range you can choose (500–6,000 ml) are app settings. They are not personalised recommendations or a safe amount for everyone.

Check with a healthcare professional before following an intake goal, especially if you have been told to limit fluids, have a kidney, heart, liver or other medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or take medicine that affects fluid balance. Drinking too much water can be harmful. If you feel unwell, seek medical help.

6. Reminders

Reminders are approximate and may be delayed, missed, skipped or rescheduled. Delivery depends on your app settings, notification permission, lock-screen and Do Not Disturb settings, battery saving, your device and manufacturer, time changes, and whether the app has been force-stopped. If you force-stop the app, open it again so it can reschedule reminders. When the skip-at-goal option is on, reminders stop for the day once your goal is met. Do not rely on My Nudge for medical or time-critical schedules.
